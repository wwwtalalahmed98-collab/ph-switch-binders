Twenty designs from two independent routes, ten each, ranked on ESM2 pseudo-log-likelihood. No design here is backed by a working prediction of binding, and we report that rather than a number we cannot stand behind.

TARGET. 7OW6 (G12D) and 7OW5 (wild type). Three states built on one shared background that differ only in the peptide: G12D, wild-type, and apo. An automated check asserts they are identical outside the peptide and that residue 6 carries the Asp side chain only in the G12D state. Epitope focus is peptide position 6 plus the 18 heavy-chain residues within 8 A of it, computed rather than assumed.

ROUTE 1 (krasG12D_01-10), scaffold-docked. Backbones from the de novo scaffold library published with the 2025 Science pMHC-binder work, designed for OTHER pMHC targets, docked over the groove and then fully resurfaced by ProteinMPNN. We declare this provenance rather than obscure it: the sequence is ours, the fold is reused.

ROUTE 2 (krasG12D_11-20), BoltzGen. BoltzGen 1, protein-anything protocol, 120 designs, generating a 60-110 aa chain against the full three-chain complex, with binding specified on peptide residues 4-8 and not_binding on the alpha-3 domain and on beta-2 microglobulin. We report the failure that preceded it: an earlier run omitted beta-2 microglobulin from the target, and all 172 harvested designs came back AS beta-2 microglobulin, the model completing the obvious hole instead of gripping the peptide. In the run used here, 59 of 120 were still substantially a copy of a chain we supplied and 18 were antibody or TCR variable domains; all were excluded. Echoes were detected against each design's own shuffled null rather than a flat identity cut, because a short design slid along a 275-residue chain scores about 0.30 identity by chance alone.

SELECTION, applied identically to both halves. Adaptyv's published expression thresholds: instability index below 40, GRAVY below 0.4, no odd cysteine count, no hydrophobic run of six or more. The submitted twenty run instability 8.3 to 38.8 and GRAVY -0.84 to +0.05. Diversity: no two designs above 0.57 identity over the best ungapped register on the shorter sequence, and ten distinct parent backbones in the scaffold half. adaptyvbio/binder-prescreen, full screen, all arms except the USPTO corpus: 20 of 20 pass, with prior-art, design and target similarity all 0.0.

RANKING. The twenty are ordered by ESM2 pseudo-log-likelihood, reported in Adaptyv's own QC guide as the best single predictor of binding at ROC AUC 0.72, ahead of ipTM at 0.64 and PAE at 0.65. That guide writes the threshold as PLL above 0, which cannot be a raw log-likelihood since those are always negative, and does not say how it is normalised, so we name our own: the excess over the same sequence shuffled, averaged over three shuffles. All twenty score positive, from +0.04 to +0.27. An AUC of 0.72 is a weak signal; it is simply the only criterion here with a published number attached, and it is one the organisers can recompute from the sequence.

WHY THERE IS NO PREDICTED AFFINITY. We built a three-state AlphaFold screen (ColabDesign binder protocol, two models per state) and ran it twice, 300 predictions, with two controls run through the identical pipeline: the JDI T-cell receptor from 7OW6, a confirmed binder of this exact pMHC, and a shuffled sequence that cannot bind. On i-pTM the real binder scored 0.158 and the shuffle 0.134, a separation of +0.024 against a run-to-run noise of 0.022, and the real binder's own preference for G12D over wild type came out at -0.001. We then recomputed pae_interaction from the stored PAE matrices: 27.62 for the real binder against 27.43 for the shuffle, so the known binder is marginally worse, and both sit far above the standard threshold of 12. ipSAE failed on all 300 predictions. Two independent interface metrics therefore agree that this screen cannot see binding. The most likely cause is that the predictions were run without an AF2 initial guess, the protocol those thresholds were calibrated on, and we ran out of time to rebuild it that way. We withdraw the screen as a failed measurement rather than rank on an instrument we have shown cannot read. Full detail, including the earlier beta-2 microglobulin failure, is in the attached methods file.
