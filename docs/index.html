What the project found

  • Predicted interface confidence anti-correlates with reaching the residues that matter on GLP-1R — r = −0.96 (n = 11) and −0.67 (n = 15) on two separate targets.
  • Cropping a membrane receptor for tractability invented a binding cavity: 211 atoms in the real receptor against 4 in the crop, at the depth designs were reaching.
  • A pH ranking metric correlated +0.24 — the wrong sign — and was rebuilt on interface burial (−0.58) before anything was submitted.
